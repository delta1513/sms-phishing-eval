Is this text message a scam?

A scam is a message from a stranger built to defraud the recipient: fake prizes and winnings, fake account, card or delivery problems, impersonation of a company or authority, premium-rate bait, or a demand for money, card details or personal information.

A normal personal message between people who know each other is not a scam, however strange, blunt, flirtatious, emotional, mundane or badly written it may be. That includes terse notes and reminders, affectionate or intimate messages, talk of illness, doctors or family, prank and wind-up texts, forwarded jokes and inspirational quotes, and a polite request from a contact or colleague for an address, a document or a bank account number.

Advertising is not a scam either. A real business promoting a real product or service, such as local trades, pest control, property, retail or health products, is only unwanted marketing.

Weigh whether the sender's purpose is deception, not how commercial, pushy or poorly written the message looks.
